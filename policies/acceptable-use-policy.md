# Acceptable Use & Secure Online Resource Policy

**COOKIE-BANNER.CA**
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** April 10, 2026
**Next Review Date:** April 10, 2027
**Owner:** Founder / Security Lead

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of COOKIE-BANNER.CA systems, accounts, and online resources. It establishes rules for secure behavior when using the internet, email, and third-party services in the course of business operations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the founder and any future employees, contractors, or third parties with access to COOKIE-BANNER.CA systems or data.

## 3. Acceptable Use of Systems

### 3.1 Authorized Use

- Company systems and accounts are for business purposes.
- All access must comply with the Information Security Policy, including MFA and least-privilege requirements.
- Systems must only be accessed from authorized, secured devices.

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities

- Using company credentials on personal or non-work accounts.
- Storing customer data outside approved systems (Supabase, Vercel, Stripe).
- Sharing credentials via email, chat, or any unencrypted channel.
- Installing unauthorized software on devices used to access production systems.
- Disabling security controls (firewall, encryption, auto-updates, MFA).

## 4. Secure Online Resource Use

### 4.1 Web Browsing

- Verify URLs before entering credentials — check for HTTPS and correct domain.
- Do not click links in unsolicited emails or messages.
- Do not download software from unverified sources.
- Keep browsers updated to the latest version with auto-updates enabled.
- Disable unnecessary browser extensions, especially those requesting broad permissions.

### 4.2 Email Security

- Do not open attachments from unknown or unexpected senders.
- Verify sender identity before acting on requests involving credentials, payments, or data.
- Report suspicious emails to security@cookie-banner.ca.
- Do not send customer data, credentials, or API keys via email.

### 4.3 Password Management

- Use a dedicated password manager (e.g., 1Password, Bitwarden) for all work accounts.
- Generate unique, random passwords of at least 16 characters for each account.
- Never reuse passwords across services.
- Never share passwords with anyone, including colleagues or support staff.
- Enable MFA on every service that supports it.

### 4.4 Third-Party Services & SaaS Tools

- New third-party tools that will process customer data must be assessed per the Information Security Policy (Section 7) before adoption.
- Free-tier tools with unclear privacy practices must not be used for any data classified as Restricted or Confidential.
- OAuth connections to third-party apps must be reviewed periodically and revoked when no longer needed.

### 4.5 Social Engineering Awareness

- Be suspicious of unsolicited contacts requesting credentials, system access, or urgent action.
- Verify requests through a separate communication channel before taking action.
- Never provide credentials or MFA codes to anyone claiming to be from a service provider — legitimate providers will never ask for these.

## 5. Reporting Violations

Any suspected violation of this policy, or any suspicious online activity, should be reported immediately to security@cookie-banner.ca. Reporting is confidential and no retaliation will occur for good-faith reports.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed annually or upon any security incident related to online resource use.
