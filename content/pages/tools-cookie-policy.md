The generator on this page needs JavaScript. This markdown page is the readable version of what it does.

## What it produces

A cookie policy that lists:

- Cookies found on the site (or cookies you enter manually)
- First-party vs third-party vendors
- Categories: necessary, functional, analytics, marketing
- Why each category is used and how long cookies last
- How visitors can accept, reject, or change preferences

Written to satisfy GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, and Quebec Law 25 disclosure rules.

## How to use it

1. Open https://www.cookie-banner.ca/tools/cookie-policy
2. Enter your website URL so the scanner can detect cookies, or add them by hand.
3. Confirm company name and privacy-policy URL.
4. Generate the policy and copy it onto your site.

Typical time: about two minutes. No signup required to generate.

## After you publish

Keep the policy in sync with a fresh [cookie scan](https://www.cookie-banner.ca/tools/cookie-scanner) whenever you add analytics, ads, or chat widgets. Pair it with a banner from https://www.cookie-banner.ca so the policy matches actual blocking behavior.
