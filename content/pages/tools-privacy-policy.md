The form on this page is interactive. This markdown page explains the tool so agents can describe it without filling the wizard.

## What it produces

A privacy policy covering:

- GDPR lawful basis, data-subject rights, transfers, and cookies
- PIPEDA accountability and consent
- CCPA/CPRA “Do Not Sell or Share” and consumer rights
- Quebec Law 25 privacy-officer and data-access sections

You can generate in English or French (Québec / Canada).

## How to use it

1. Open https://www.cookie-banner.ca/tools/privacy-policy
2. Enter business name, location, contact email, and the laws that apply.
3. List the data you collect and the third-party tools you use.
4. Generate the policy.
5. Free account: copy HTML or download and host it on your site.
6. Pro: publish a hosted URL such as `https://www.cookie-banner.ca/p/your-business/privacy-policy` with last-updated timestamps and version history.

No credit card is required to generate a draft.

## When to update

Update the policy when you add a new tracker, change retention, or start selling in a new region. Review at least annually.
